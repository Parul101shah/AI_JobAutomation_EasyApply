How it works

  1. Profile: register (POST /api/users/register) or load by ID (GET /api/users/{id}).
  2. Resume: upload a PDF and AI extracts skills (POST /api/users/{id}/upload-resume). Review it, then confirm (POST /api/users/{id}/confirm-profile).
  3. Discover: search Greenhouse + Lever boards (POST /api/jobs/discovery/run).
  4. Match: AI scores jobs against your profile (POST /api/jobs/match/{userId}).
  5. Notify: HIGH matches are emailed to you (POST /api/jobs/match/{userId}/notify).
  6. Email links: build a tailored resume (/api/resumes/confirm/{token}) and auto-apply (/api/applications/apply/{token}).
  7. My Resumes: list and download generated PDFs (GET /api/resumes/user/{id}, /api/resumes/{id}/pdf).
